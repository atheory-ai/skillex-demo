## Use When

Use when building operational overview pages.

## Do

- Lead with PageHeader and high-signal status.
- Put summary metrics near the top.
- Keep actions close to the status they affect.

## Do Not

- Turn dashboards into marketing surfaces.
- Hide the key state below low-priority content.

## Example

A campaign dashboard should show active status before secondary metrics.
