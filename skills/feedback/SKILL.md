## Use When

Use when communicating success, warning, or critical state.

## Do

- Match tone to user impact.
- Keep feedback close to the affected workflow.
- Use action links only when they advance the current task.

## Do Not

- Treat all banners as promotional.
- Use critical tone for routine state.

## Example

An active campaign notice may be success in admin and warning in storefront if availability is limited.
