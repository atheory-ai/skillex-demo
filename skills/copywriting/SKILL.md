## Use When

Use when writing labels, headings, banners, or helper text.

## Do

- Keep operational copy concise and factual.
- Keep storefront copy customer-facing and benefit-led.
- Prefer specific nouns over generic status phrases.

## Do Not

- Reuse admin incident language in customer flows.
- Treat brand tone as interchangeable across apps.

## Example

"Campaign is active in 5 regions" fits admin; "15 percent off spring bundles" fits storefront.
