## Use When

Use when deciding whether a style belongs in an app or package.

## Do

- Keep app shells, grids, and section layout in app CSS.
- Keep reusable component styling in packages.
- Use tone maps and spacing scales consistently.

## Do Not

- Push app-specific styles into shared primitives.
- Rebuild a shared primitive just to change page layout.

## Example

Product grids belong in storefront CSS; Banner tones belong in the design system.

## See Also

Use private package styling skills when editing shared components.
