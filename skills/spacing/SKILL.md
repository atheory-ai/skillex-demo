## Use When

Use when deciding how much visual separation sections need.

## Do

- Keep related controls close together.
- Leave clear separation between page header, feedback, and content.
- Prefer shared layout primitives over one-off margins.

## Do Not

- Encode arbitrary margins into copied snippets.
- Let banners or summaries crowd the content they explain.

## Example

Page-level sections usually need larger spacing than field groups.
