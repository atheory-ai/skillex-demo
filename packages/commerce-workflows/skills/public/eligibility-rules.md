## Use When

Use when workflow output depends on region or campaign scope.

## Do

- Keep eligibility phrasing short and customer-readable.
- Use workflow output to centralize repeated conditions.
- Let the app decide where the message appears.

## Do Not

- Duplicate eligibility wording across multiple pages.
- Turn the workflow package into a UI layer.

## Example

Use one workflow output to mention offer availability in US and Canada.
