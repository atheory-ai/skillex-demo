## Use When

Use when selecting `info`, `success`, `warning`, or `critical`.

## Do

- Choose tone from user impact.
- Reserve `critical` for blocking or urgent issues.
- Keep copy aligned with the chosen tone.

## Do Not

- Use tone as decoration.
- Treat promotional offers as critical.

## Example

Seasonal offers often fit `warning`; confirmed benefits can fit `success`.
