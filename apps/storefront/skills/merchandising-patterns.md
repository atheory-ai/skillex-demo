## Use When

Use for storefront home and promotions pages.

## Do

- Write customer-facing copy around benefits and urgency.
- Use v3 Banner slots so content and actions are explicit.
- Place merchandising messages near the product or campaign context.

## Do Not

- Use admin incident or workflow language.
- Collapse Banner content into v2 convenience props.

## Example

Use `Banner.Title` for the offer and `Banner.Description` for eligibility or terms.
