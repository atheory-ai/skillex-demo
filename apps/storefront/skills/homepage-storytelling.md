## Use When

Use when writing or arranging content for the storefront home page.

## Do

- Lead with seasonal or category context.
- Tie promotional messaging to tangible customer benefits.
- Keep the supporting product content visible nearby.

## Do Not

- Use dashboard-style status language.
- Hide the merchandised products beneath abstract copy.

## Example

Frame the season or routine first, then support it with a promotion banner.
