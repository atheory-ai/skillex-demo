## Use When

Use when a message needs to reduce hesitation or clarify an offer.

## Do

- Use short factual qualifiers.
- Keep confidence-building language close to the purchase context.
- Support the main benefit rather than replacing it.

## Do Not

- Use internal incident or compliance wording.
- Over-explain routine conditions.

## Example

Mention shipping eligibility or region scope in the Banner description.
