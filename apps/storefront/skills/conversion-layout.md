## Use When

Use when arranging promotional content and product context.

## Do

- Keep the offer close to the product or category it affects.
- Give the primary CTA clear visual priority.
- Use compositional spacing that feels lighter than admin.

## Do Not

- Reuse dense operational layouts from admin.
- Separate campaign copy from the action it supports.

## Example

A promotions page can place the Banner above the product grid with one CTA.
