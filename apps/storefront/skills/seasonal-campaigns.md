## Use When

Use when the promotion has seasonal context.

## Do

- Tie the campaign to a recognizable season or customer routine.
- Keep the visual tone current and customer-facing.
- Pair the campaign with one clear shopping action.

## Do Not

- Use internal campaign-management language.
- Mention backend workflow details in customer copy.

## Example

"Spring reset" works as a customer-facing campaign name.
