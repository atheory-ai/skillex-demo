## Use When

Use when an offer has region, eligibility, or time constraints.

## Do

- Keep the primary benefit in the title.
- Put short qualification or terms language in supporting copy.
- Mention region or eligibility when it affects the offer materially.

## Do Not

- Hide important limits behind generic CTA copy.
- Turn disclaimers into legalistic paragraphs unless required.

## Example

"Available now for customers in US and Canada" is enough for a demo promotion.
