## Use When

Use when a page offers one primary next step.

## Do

- Keep one dominant customer-facing CTA.
- Use secondary actions only when they preserve shopping context.
- Match CTA labels to customer intent.

## Do Not

- Overload promotional surfaces with internal-style actions.
- Use vague labels like "Proceed" when the destination is known.

## Example

"Shop promotions" is clearer than "Continue" on a merchandising surface.
