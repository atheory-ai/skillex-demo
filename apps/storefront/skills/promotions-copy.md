## Use When

Use when writing Banner and PageHeader copy for storefront promotions.

## Do

- Lead with the customer benefit.
- Keep the description concrete and short.
- Use action language that matches shopping behavior.

## Do Not

- Use admin approval or escalation terminology.
- Phrase promotions like internal status updates.

## Example

"15 percent off bundles" is stronger than "Campaign active."
