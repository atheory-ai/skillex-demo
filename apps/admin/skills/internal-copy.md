## Use When

Use when writing headings, labels, or helper text in admin.

## Do

- Be direct and operational.
- Name the system object being changed.
- Use verbs that match internal workflows.

## Do Not

- Use customer-facing sales language.
- Hide business impact behind vague labels.

## Example

"Escalation email" is better than "Contact preference" in admin settings.
