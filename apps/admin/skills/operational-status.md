## Use When

Use for summaries of approvals, incidents, or campaign state.

## Do

- Lead with the most actionable status.
- Pair status with a next step when intervention is possible.
- Keep metrics secondary to state.

## Do Not

- Bury active issues below descriptive prose.
- Use marketing-style reassurance language.

## Example

Show campaign state before region metrics.
