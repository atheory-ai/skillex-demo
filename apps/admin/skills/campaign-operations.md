## Use When

Use for campaign dashboards, settings, and operational notices.

## Do

- Prefer precise status and region counts.
- Keep action copy tied to internal workflows.
- Surface issues before supporting metrics.

## Do Not

- Phrase admin controls as storefront offers.
- Use decorative campaign language in operational pages.

## Example

"Campaign is active in 5 regions" is clearer than generic success text.
